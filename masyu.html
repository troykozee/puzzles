How to play
  • Draw a single closed loop through the centers of cells. It moves side to side or up and down, and never branches or crosses itself.
  • The loop passes through every pearl.
  • On a white pearl the loop goes straight, and it turns in the cell just before or just after the pearl, or both.
  • On a black pearl the loop turns, then runs straight through the next cell on both sides.
  • Drag from cell to cell to draw. Drag along an existing line to erase it.
  • Tap the gap between two cells to cycle line, X, empty. Right-click a gap to place an X where you know the loop doesn’t go.
  • Keyboard: arrows move, Shift+arrow draws or erases, Alt+arrow places an X, Ctrl+Z undoes.

A pearl turns green once the loop around it follows its rule.
