How to play
  • Each number sits in an island of exactly that many cells.
  • Every island holds one number, and islands never touch side to side.
  • All shaded cells form one connected stream.
  • The stream never fills a 2×2 square.
  • Click or tap a cell to cycle: stream, dot, empty.
  • Right-click to place a dot. Drag to paint several cells.
  • Keyboard: arrows move, Space cycles, S stream, D dot, Backspace clears, Ctrl+Z undoes.

Dots are optional notes for cells you know are island. A number turns green once its island is walled off at the right size.
